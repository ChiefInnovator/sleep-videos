# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People who want steady background sound to fall asleep, rest, or wind down at night: white noise, brown noise, rain, distant thunder, or an ambient drone. They arrive curious about one sound, want to hear it before committing to eight hours, and will listen in a dark room, usually on a phone or a laptop at low volume.

## Product Purpose

Sleep Soundscapes by Inventing Fire with AI is a collection of five original eight-hour sleep videos: softened white noise, distant thunder and rain, gentle rain on a window, deep brown noise, and a deep ambient sleep drone. Each opens with a six-second title, then shows a black screen.

The site is a **funnel to the YouTube videos and channel**. Visitors preview a sound and are sent on to watch the full video on YouTube. Direct MP4 downloads (GitHub Release v1.0.0) are secondary. Success means visitors who click through to YouTube.

Open: no videos are on YouTube yet, so no channel or video URLs exist. Until they do, the full-length path is the GitHub Release download.

## Positioning

Every sound is generated by original procedural synthesis for this collection: continuous over eight hours, not a looped clip, with no recordings, samples, or third-party audio. Each video's provenance, seed, loudness, and frame-level checks are published in its production report.

## Operating Context

- Listening happens at bedtime, in low light, often on a phone in bed.
- Previews are three minutes long; full videos are 8:00:00, 1280×720, black after 00:06.
- Playback starts only on a user action; choosing another sound pauses the current one.
- The site is a static page (`site/`) published on GitHub Pages, with no build step or third-party JavaScript.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Five sounds, each with a three-minute preview in `site/media/` and an eight-hour MP4 on GitHub Releases.
- Audio fades: a two-second fade-in and a 25-second fade-out, in previews and full videos alike.
- The current copy makes no health or sleep-outcome claims, makes no premium or comparative-quality claims, and says the rain and thunder are synthesized. The owner has not declared these binding brand commitments; changing any of them is a product decision to raise with the owner, not a copy edit.
- Critical listening review is pending (`reports/listening-review.md`).

## Brand Commitments

- Published as "Sleep Soundscapes by Inventing Fire with AI".
- The Inventing Fire with AI flame mark (`assets/inventing-fire-title.png`, adapted from the owner's brand artwork) appears in every video's opening and is part of the identity.

## Evidence on Hand

- Five preview videos and title posters: `site/media/`.
- Production sheets, measurements and hashes: `reports/*.json`, `reports/full-summary.md`.
- Draft YouTube titles and descriptions: `youtube-metadata.md`.
- Absent, and must not be fabricated: testimonials, listener counts, YouTube metrics, reviews, completed listening results, sleep-outcome evidence.

## Product Principles

1. The sound is the product. Everything on the page helps someone pick a sound and hear it.
2. Say only what has been verified. The page reflects the production reports, not aspirations.
3. Respect the bedtime context: nothing plays, flashes, or gets louder without a tap.
4. Lead to the full experience. Every preview should make the next step to the eight-hour video obvious.
