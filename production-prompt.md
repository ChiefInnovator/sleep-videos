### Audio engineering and verification

- Work from lossless sources and maintain a **48 kHz, 24-bit stereo master**.
- Render a high-quality YouTube-compatible video with high-bitrate audio. Retain the lossless master separately.
- Measure integrated loudness, loudness variation, and true peak. Choose a conservative, consistent loudness appropriate to each sound; do not maximize loudness for impact.
- Leave encoding headroom, targeting a maximum true peak of **−2 dBTP**.
- Document the final measurements. Playback volume still depends on the listener’s device.
- Build extended soundscapes with enough source variation to prevent recognizable short loops. Use seamless transitions without phase cancellation or level dips.
- Analyze the full rendered files for clipping, dropouts, unintended silence, abrupt level changes, and duration errors.
- Confirm that every video frame is completely black.
- Listen critically to the opening, ending, every major transition, and distributed samples throughout all eight hours.
- Compare representative excerpts against available reference excerpts at matched perceived loudness. Assess realism, harshness, repetition, artifacts, and comfort. Do not claim a comparison was completed if the references were inaccessible.
- If an authorized YouTube test upload is available, check the transcoded playback for audible degradation.
